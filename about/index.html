Hi, I'm Sihao Chen.

I'm an illustrator and printmaker in New Jersey, and a 2026 SVA Illustration graduate.

Carving stamps was a quiet hobby of mine in middle school. I cut little eraser stamps from pictures I found online. They weren't very good, and I put the knives away for years.

In college I found what I love: line, and catching a shape by its silhouette. My thesis was a tarot deck, made digitally but built like a print.

At school I studied etching and screen printing, but class conflicts kept me from ever taking woodcut. After graduating I wanted to carve at home, and woodcut felt like a lot for a small space, so my thesis professor suggested linocut. It let me build a little studio right at home, and I haven't stopped since.

I never expected so many people to watch my carving videos. Thank you for following along. It makes me really happy!!!!

Now I design my own images, then carve and print every stamp and print here by hand.
