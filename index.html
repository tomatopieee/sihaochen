Process

Two blocks, one gnome.

The gnome bookmark is printed from two carved blocks: one for the color, one for the black lines. Each pass is lined up by hand, so no two prints come out the same.

Etchings, linocuts, ceramics and illustration are moving to this site soon. Until then the newest work is on Instagram.

About

I'm Sihao Chen, an illustrator and printmaker. I carve rubber stamps and linoleum blocks, pull etchings, and make ceramics. Most of what I make starts as a joke or a meme and ends up very carefully carved.
