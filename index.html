Process

Carved, inked, pulled by hand.

Every postcard and bookmark is printed from a block I carved myself. The gnome bookmark takes two blocks, one for the color and one for the black lines, lined up by hand. No two prints come out the same.

About

I'm Sihao Chen, an illustrator and printmaker. I carve rubber stamps and linoleum blocks, pull etchings, and make ceramics. Most of what I make starts as a joke or a meme and ends up very carefully carved.
